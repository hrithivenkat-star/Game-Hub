How To Survive

  • Move — WASD / Arrow Keys on desktop, on-screen joystick on mobile
  • Watch the chef — a warning flash means he's about to turn. Freeze the instant he does.
  • Shadows on the floor mean a pot is about to fall — don't stand under one.
  • Golden cheese is worth 5x a normal piece — but it's not worth dying for.
  • Every minute survived, enemies get faster and meaner.
